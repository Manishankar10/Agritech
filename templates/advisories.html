Tamil Nadu Government Agricultural Schemes & Subsidies
Micro-Irrigation Scheme (PMKSY - சொட்டு நீர் பாசனம்)
TN Horticulture & Agriculture Dept

100% Subsidy for Small and Marginal Farmers (up to 5 acres). 75% Subsidy for other farmers. Covers Drip and Sprinkler irrigation kits to conserve water.

100% Subsidy Apply at Block AO / Uzhavan App
Free Agricultural Electricity (இலவச விவசாய மின்சாரம்)
TANGEDCO / Government of Tamil Nadu

24x7 free three-phase agricultural power supply for registered agricultural pumpsets across all districts. Fast-track tatkal connections available.

100% Free Power TANGEDCO Section Office
PM-KISAN & CM Uzhavar Pathukappu Thittam
Direct Benefit Transfer

₹6,000 per year in 3 equal installments of ₹2,000 directly to farmer bank accounts. Chief Minister's Uzhavar Pathukappu Thittam covers social security, accidental relief, and education aid for farmer families.

₹6,000 / Year pmkisan.gov.in
Crop Insurance (PMFBY - பயிர் காப்பீட்டுத் திட்டம்)
Agricultural Insurance Company of India

Specialized premium rates (1.5% for Rabi/Samba, 2% for Kharif) with state government subsidy. Protects against cyclone, unseasonal rain, drought, and pest epidemics.
