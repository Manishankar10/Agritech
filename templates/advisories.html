Tamil Nadu Government Agricultural Schemes & Subsidies
Micro-Irrigation Subsidy (PMKSY - சொட்டு நீர் பாசனம்)
100% Subsidy
TN Horticulture & Agriculture Department

100% Subsidy for Small and Marginal Farmers (up to 5 acres). 75% Subsidy for other farmers. Covers Drip and Sprinkler irrigation systems to conserve ground water.

Apply at Block Assistant Director of Agriculture (ADA) / Uzhavan App
Free Agricultural Electricity (இலவச விவசாய மின்சாரம்)
100% Free Power
TANGEDCO / Government of Tamil Nadu

24x7 free three-phase agricultural electricity supply for registered agricultural pumpsets across all districts. Fast-track tatkal connections available.

Apply at local TANGEDCO Section Office
PM-KISAN & CM Uzhavar Pathukappu Thittam
₹6,000 / Year
Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)

₹6,000 per year in 3 equal installments of ₹2,000 directly to farmer bank accounts. Chief Minister's Uzhavar Pathukappu Thittam covers accidental relief and educational assistance.

pmkisan.gov.in & Village Administrative Officer (VAO)
Crop Insurance (PMFBY - பயிர் காப்பீட்டுத் திட்டம்)
Risk Protection
Agricultural Insurance Company of India

Subsidized premium rates (1.5% for Samba/Rabi, 2% for Kuruvai/Kharif). Comprehensive risk protection against cyclone, drought, unseasonal rainfall, and pest epidemics.
